Stick figure

A stick figure drawn with lines and a circle.

A stick figure is a very simple drawing of a person or animal, composed of a few lines, curves, and dots. In a stick figure, the head is represented by a circle, sometimes embellished with details such as eyes, a mouth or crudely scratched-out hair. The arms, legs and torso are usually represented by straight lines.

Stick figures are often drawn by children, and appear in animation, comics and signs. Their simplicity makes them easy to draw quickly and to recognise at a glance, which is why they are used in safety signs, instructions and diagrams all over the world.

History

Early examples are found in rock paintings and petroglyphs from many parts of the world. They have stayed in use ever since, from medieval marginalia to modern web comics and short animated films.
